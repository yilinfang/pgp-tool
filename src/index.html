PGP Tool

Everything runs in the page. Nothing is sent or stored. Each page is separate: when you go to another page or close the tab, the page forgets all keys.

  • Generate a new key pair.
  • Encrypt a message to public keys, and optionally sign it.
  • Decrypt a message with your private key, and verify its signature.
  • Sign a message as a cleartext signature.
  • Verify a signed message.
  • Revoke a public key with its revocation certificate.
