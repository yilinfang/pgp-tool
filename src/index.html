PGP Tool

An offline OpenPGP tool, built on OpenPGP.js. Generate keys, encrypt, decrypt, sign and verify messages, and revoke keys, with no installation and no GnuPG. The keys and messages work with GnuPG and other OpenPGP software.

  • Generate a new key pair.
  • Encrypt a message to public keys, and optionally sign it.
  • Decrypt a message with your private key, and verify its signature.
  • Sign a message as a cleartext signature.
  • Verify a signed message.
  • Revoke a public key with its revocation certificate.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent over the internet or saved.
